简介About

我的主要研究方向是变异社会语言学，研究兴趣主要集中于语言与身份、语言与媒体、社会语音学，以及二语习得中的社会语言变异习得与社会语言能力发展。近三年的研究主要关注社会因素对汉语语音变异的影响、汉语语音在各类媒体中的应用与变异情况，以及汉语作为第二语言学习者的社会语言变异习得与感知。 My work sits in sociolinguistic variation, with interests in language and identity, language and media, sociophonetics, and the intersection of second language acquisition and sociolinguistics — in particular the acquisition of sociolinguistic variation and the development of sociolinguistic competence. Over the past three years I have focused on how social factors shape variation in Chinese (Mandarin) pronunciation, how Chinese speech is used and varies across media, and how learners of Chinese as a second language acquire and perceive sociolinguistic variation.
